# 2.2.2-2 Switch 負責連接 LAN 內設備

> [!info] 藍色良品筆記：資訊塊
> 本筆記由紅色資訊源 [[01_紅色良品筆記_資訊源/CCNA-V1-CH2-15_red_note#2.2.2-2 Switch 負責連接 LAN 內設備|2.2.2-2 Switch 負責連接 LAN 內設備]] 拆出。

## 原文內容

The role of a switch is to connect devices within a LAN. For example, all of the PCs, security cameras, printers, servers, and other devices in an office are probably connected to one or more switches. For this reason, it's common for switches to have many ports for end hosts to connect to—usually from 24 to 48 per switch.

## 逐句繁體中文翻譯

- Switch 的角色是連接 LAN 內的設備。
- 例如，office 中所有 PCs、security cameras、printers、servers，以及其他設備，很可能都連接到一台或多台 switches。
- 因此，switches 通常會有很多 ports 供 end hosts 連接；每台 switch 通常有 24 到 48 個 ports。

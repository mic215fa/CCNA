# 1.1.3-0 CCNA 可在線上或考場應試

> [!info] 藍色良品筆記：資訊塊
> 本筆記由紅色資訊源 [[01_紅色良品筆記_資訊源/CCNA-V1-CH2-15_red_note#1.1.3-0 CCNA 可在線上或考場應試|1.1.3-0 CCNA 可在線上或考場應試]] 拆出。

## 原文內容

The CCNA exam, administered by Cisco's testing partner Pearson VUE, can be taken either at an authorized test center or online. To schedule the exam, visit CertMetrics (https://cp.certmetrics.com/cisco/en/login). If you don't have a Cisco account yet, you'll have to make one; just click Sign Up, and make an account.

## 逐句繁體中文翻譯

- CCNA 考試由 Cisco 的測驗合作夥伴 Pearson VUE 管理，可以在授權考場應試，也可以線上應試。
- 若要預約考試，請前往 CertMetrics：`https://cp.certmetrics.com/cisco/en/login`。
- 如果你還沒有 Cisco account，需要先建立一個；只要點 Sign Up 並建立帳號即可。

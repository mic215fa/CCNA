# 2.2.4-0 兩個 LAN 可透過 Internet 通訊

> [!info] 藍色良品筆記：資訊塊
> 本筆記由紅色資訊源 [[01_紅色良品筆記_資訊源/CCNA-V1-CH2-15_red_note#2.2.4-0 兩個 LAN 可透過 Internet 通訊|2.2.4-0 兩個 LAN 可透過 Internet 通訊]] 拆出。

## 原文內容

Devices in the two LANs in figure 2.5 are perfectly capable of communicating with each other and with other devices over the internet. However, by allowing our devices to communicate over the internet, we are exposing them to potential security risks.

## 逐句繁體中文翻譯

- Figure 2.5 中兩個 LANs 裡的設備，完全可以彼此通訊，也可以和 internet 上的其他設備通訊。
- 不過，當我們允許設備透過 internet 通訊時，也會讓它們暴露在潛在 security risks 中。
